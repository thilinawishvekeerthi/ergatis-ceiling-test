Talent Management Module 
 
Managing talent is a continuous process that involves several stages, from capturing an employee’s 
career aspirations to updating development plans. The whole process should be carefully handled. 
Talent Management module provides a comprehensive solution by automating each phase of the 
process. It is an easy-to-use system that can be utilized by employees belonging to different work 
categories, such as employees, reviewers, supervisors, review board members and HR 
administrators. 
 
Lifecycle of Talent Management Module 
The lifecycle of the Talent Management module contains 4 phases, which repeat as a continuous 
cycle. 
 
 
 
Figure 1: Lifecycle of Talent Management Module 
 
 
 
1. Capture employee’s Career Aspirations 
The employees enter their career aspiration details such as mobility details, preferred 
jobs, long-term career goals and preferred workstation details. 
2. Review Talents to identify Top Performers/Future Leaders 
In this stage employees are assessed by the assigned reviewer and talent review 
board in order to identify the top performers/future leaders in the organization. 
3. Update the Succession Plan 
In this stage talent review board plans about when and how the succession will take place 
in the organization.
